Client

Industry

Goal

Generate organic sales and turn content into a consistent client acquisition channel

Challenge

Active page with no clear content direction, low engagement, and zero inbound leads despite consistent posting

During our onboarding I asked: "Who exactly are you trying to reach with your content?"

The client responded: "Women who want better relationships and want to attract quality men."

While this seemed clear, it revealed a deeper problem — the content wasn't speaking to that woman's pain points or buying triggers. Without content built around what your ideal client is feeling, your page gets views but never converts.

My Strategy & Approach

Week 3 – 4

  • Began content rollout with conversion-focused posts
  • Wrote short-form video scripts with strong hooks and direct CTAs
  • Focused on trust-building content before pushing the offer
  • Maintained consistent posting and daily audience engagement
